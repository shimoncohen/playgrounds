# Code of Conduct

Playgrounds exists to help people learn. Beginners are our audience, so patience with beginners is our culture.

## Our standard

- Be kind, curious and welcoming. Assume good faith.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Answer questions without making anyone feel small.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, languages and ways of learning.
- Keep discussion on topic; no harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised content, doxxing or spam.
- Contributions made with AI tools are welcome. The human who submits one is responsible for it and treats reviewers with the same respect.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs, and may block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the item, or email the maintainer at the address on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

This is a short statement in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which applies where this text is silent.
